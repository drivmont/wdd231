Discover Santa Cruz

Santa Cruz at a Glance

  • Founded: 1561 by Spanish explorer Ñuflo de Chávez
  • Population: Bolivia's most populous city
  • Elevation: about 416 m (1,365 ft)
  • Climate: warm and tropical, averaging about 24 °C
  • Economy: agribusiness, energy, commerce and services
  • Region: capital of Santa Cruz, Bolivia's largest department

Join the Santa Cruz Chamber of Commerce

Joining the Santa Cruz Chamber of Commerce opens doors to a world of opportunities for your business. Connect with local businesses, gain access to exclusive events, and be part of a thriving community that supports growth and collaboration.

Membership benefits include:

  • Networking opportunities with local businesses
  • Access to business resources and support
  • Promotion through our member directory
  • Invitations to exclusive events and workshops

To become a member, please fill out the application form below or contact us for more information.

Cost: $30 per month
